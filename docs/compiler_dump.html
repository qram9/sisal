IF1 dump — a rebound name in a nested for-initial

Two identical loops. The only difference is whether the inner initial clause reuses the enclosing name w or picks a fresh one v. The shadowed version is 24 IF1 lines shorter, and what is missing is a carry.

6 entries — W still IMPORTED from (0,3)

The two halves disagree: the symtab entry is repointed at the local node, but the BOUNDARY still lists the name as imported from the enclosing scope, and no MERGE pair is created for the local binding. The inner loop is therefore short one carry and its OLD, LoopB ends up with fewer slots than INIT exports, and the positional carry derivation downstream reads the wrong window — which is the scrambled seeding seen in the emitted C.
