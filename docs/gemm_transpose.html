GEMM with Transpose

The equation

BLAS GEMM (general matrix–matrix multiply) computes:

$$ C \;\leftarrow\; \alpha\,\operatorname{op}(A)\,\operatorname{op}(B) \;+\; \beta\,C $$

where each operand can independently be transposed:

$$ \operatorname{op}(X) = \begin{cases} X & \text{NoTrans} \\ X^{\mathsf T} & \text{Trans} \\ X^{\mathsf H} & \text{ConjTrans} \end{cases} $$

Element-wise:

$$ C_{ij} \;\leftarrow\; \alpha \sum_{k=1}^{K} \operatorname{op}(A)_{ik}\,\operatorname{op}(B)_{kj} \;+\; \beta\,C_{ij} $$

with the shape contract: $\operatorname{op}(A)$ is $M\times K$,   $\operatorname{op}(B)$ is $K\times N$,   $C$ is $M\times N$.

The CBLAS call:

cblas_dgemm(order, transA, transB, M, N, K, alpha, A, lda, B, ldb, beta, C, ldc);

For $C = A\cdot A^{\mathsf T}$  (A is $n\times m$)

We want $C$ ($n\times n$) with $\operatorname{op}(A)=A$, $\operatorname{op}(B)=A^{\mathsf T}$, and B is the same buffer as A:

giving

$$ C_{ij} = \sum_{k=1}^{m} A_{ik}\,(A^{\mathsf T})_{kj} = \sum_{k=1}^{m} A_{ik}\,A_{jk}. $$

How the transpose is actually realized

transB = Trans does not rearrange any data. It tells GEMM to index the same buffer differently:

$$ \operatorname{op}(B)_{kj} = \begin{cases} B[\,k\cdot ldb + j\,] & \text{NoTrans} \\[4pt] B[\,j\cdot ldb + k\,] & \text{Trans} \end{cases} $$

So the transpose is just swapping which index is multiplied by the leading dimension ($ldb$) when computing the address.

The key insight. The leading dimension lda/ldb is a stride, and the trans flag picks the traversal order. GEMM already lets you describe a transposed matrix as (buffer, leading-dimension, trans-flag) with zero copy — exactly the strided dope-vector idea in miniature.

Connection to the dope vector (sisal_array_t)

  • lda/ldb  =  the stride field we'd add to sisal_array_t (which today carries only dims[8], i.e. contiguous row-major).
  • transA/transB  =  "are the strides swapped?"
  • If the DV carried strides, feeding A.T to GEMM would be automatic: map the DV's stride to ldb and its transpose-ness to transB, and BLAS does the rest — no materialization of $A^{\mathsf T}$.
